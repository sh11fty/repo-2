Never lose a job to an expired ticket.

A simple Android app for sole-trader tradespeople to track licences, cards and insurance renewals.

  • Snap a photo of each certificate, card or insurance schedule and keep it in one place.
  • Get reminders 90, 30 and 7 days before anything expires.
  • Share a tidy compliance pack with clients when they ask for proof.

Planned price: Free for 3 documents; about £2.99/month for unlimited (subject to change).
